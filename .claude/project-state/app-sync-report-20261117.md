## 3. Legal (STOP — proposals, not applied)

The app's privacy policy (`docs/legal/privacy-policy.md` @ `2d33417`) was not changed by the batch, so the verbatim sync is a no-op. But the app now does things the policy does not describe. The site must not get ahead of the policy, so these are proposals for the **app repo's** policy; after they land, run `npm run sync:legal` and mirror them on `/support`.

1. **§1 What we collect** — add menu photos and booking details. Proposed, after "Photos you add.": "**Photos you add.** Dish photos (up to three per dish), place cover photos, up to ten menu photos per place, and your profile picture." and a new paragraph: "**Booking details.** For a place you added, you can save a phone number, a booking link and a picture of a booking QR code. They are private to you (see section 7)."
2. **§6 On your phone** — Today's pick keeps its on/off setting and a short list of recently picked places on the device. Proposed addition: "Today's pick keeps its setting and the places it showed in the last two weeks on your phone only, so it doesn't repeat itself."
3. **§7 Discoverable diaries** — menu photos are now shared with the place (they are in the shared view); booking details never are. Proposed: places bullet becomes "the places you added (name, category, city, address, position on the map, cover photo, menu photos) and your "recommended" answer;" and "Always private" gains "your booking details (phone number, booking link and QR picture)".
4. **§8 Export** — Proposed: "**Export it:** Profile, Settings, Export my data. The app builds a zip file on your phone (your data as JSON and HTML, your photos including menu photos, your booking details and QR pictures, and a short README) and opens the share sheet. We do not receive a copy."
5. **§8 Delete** — deletion now also removes menu photos and booking details (the delete-account function sweeps both). Proposed: "Deleting removes your account, diary, booking details, wish list, photos and profile picture from our systems."

Website follow-ups once the policy is synced (left as they are now; they remain true but incomplete): `/support#what-friends-see` (add menu photos to the shared list and booking details to "always private"), `/support#export` (booking details, README), `/support#delete-account`, and the landing privacy promise "Take it with you". Recorded in TASKS.md.
